Breaker Box Directory

The night a breaker half-trips is the worst time to learn what row 9 covers. Fifteen quiet minutes now buys you a calm minute later.

  1. Open your panel, stand with a partner, and flip one breaker off at a time.
  2. Walk the house together and find what went dark: lamps, outlets, appliances.
  3. Write that room or fixture on its row, then flip it back on and move along.

Tip: circuits do not always match rooms. Gracefully accept that the bathroom and the hallway may share one line.
