Cable & Charger Tags

The mystery cord in the drawer ends today. Tag every cable, charger, and dongle once, and you will never guess again.

  1. Start with the cords you reach for most (phone, tablet, laptop chargers).
  2. Cut a tag, write the device name on the line, and fold it around the cord.
  3. Tape it shut with a small piece of clear tape or a labeled clip.

Tip: write what it plugs INTO, not what it plugs FROM. "Charging brick" is not the helpful part. "Phone" is.
