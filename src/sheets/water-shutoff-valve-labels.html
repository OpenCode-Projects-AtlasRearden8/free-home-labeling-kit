Water Shutoff & Valve Labels

When a supply line bursts, you need to find the right valve in ten seconds, not ten minutes. These stickers go right on the valves themselves.

  1. Print this sheet, cut the labels apart along the dashed lines.
  2. Tape or glue-stick each one onto (or beside) the matching valve.
  3. Turn each valve once now, while the day is calm, so your hands know the move.

Shutoffs usually close clockwise ("righty-tighty"). If one does not turn, leave that one alone and call a plumber — forcing old valves can crack them.
