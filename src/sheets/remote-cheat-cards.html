Remote Control Cheat Cards

Nobody needs to memorize three remotes and an input menu. One index-card cheat sheet taped to the TV cabinet beats shouting "which button?!" every weekend.

  1. Pick a card, sit down at the TV one evening, and figure it out ONCE while you have snacks.
  2. Write only the button names and presses that actually work at your house.
  3. Tape the completed card inside the TV cabinet door for guests, kids, and future-you.

Tip: fill out the "turning it on" card first — most remote panic is really just "which button gets us back to Netflix."
